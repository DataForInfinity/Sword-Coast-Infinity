-=Blades & Bolts=-

Crossbows have not seen widespred use in my games nor did I ever met anyone who could be convinced of the Cleric's "I can't see blood" ethos. Luckily, there are plenty of remedies to choose from at this point. However, after delving into some overhauls (starting back hen with the now retired Scales of Balance) I recognize that these can be a bit much at times while the light weight options available today now feel incomplete. I hope to provide here a middle ground for when you want to stay in touch with the vanilla experience just without the irrational - if not punishing - limitations.

Compatibility note: to not mess up any modded cleric kits with specifically tailored weapon selection the mod will touch only kits with exactly the same weapon selection as the base class.

-=Components=-

Note: all item and class descriptions get properly updated. There is, however, a limitation in the EE's automatic item usability description which assumes that all clerics would be the same. Hence, differences between single and multi class Clerics may not be correctly displayed.

[100] Universal ammunition
	+ Original classes could holster only ammo for which they could also use the launcher, later additions do not face such limitations.
	  This pure quality of life component allows everyone to treat the ammo slots like an unrestricted inventory extension

[200] One-handed light crossbows
	+ Makes all light crossbows one-handed weapons, which allows them to be used with a shield
	  This idea makes perfect sense, but I could not find it anywhere without being deeply entangled in a complete overhaul
	! So here it is - mandatory (logical) basis for all the following components

[300] Enable Mages and all their kits to use and become proficient with light crossbows
	-> Mages may only use the following weapons: dagger, quarterstaff, CROSSBOW, dart, sling.
	? Strangely, mages could only use ranged weapons benefiting from high strength.
	  Crossbows on the other hand would seem to be natural choice for someone less physique focused
	  (and puts an halt to the indignity which was archmages throwing pebbles at each other...)

The following components are mutually exclusive. Pick which range of weapons your clerics feel most comfortable with.

[410] Moderate expansion: Enable Clerics and related kits to use and become proficient with light crossbows and darts
	-> Clerics may only use the following weapons: war hammer, club, flail, mace, quarterstaff, CROSSBOW, DART, sling.
	+ Warrior multiclass clerics can also use heavy crossbows
	? Pick this if you only want more ranged options to keep the number of proficiencies in line with Druids or Thiefs

[420] Generous expansion: Enable Clerics and related kits to use and become proficient with light crossbows, darts and straight blades
	-> Clerics may only use the following weapons: LONG SWORD, SHORT SWORD, DAGGER, war hammer, club, flail, mace, quarterstaff, CROSSBOW, DART, sling.
	+ Warrior multiclass clerics can also use heavy crossbows, bastard and two-handed swords
	? Pick this if you want to have fun with dual and multi classes
